### 2. Who Buys It and Who Uses It
**Who buys:** Corporate Purchasing / Head of IT Procurement (wants spend governance and early reorder lead times).  
**Who uses:** Project Leads (daily steering and approving work logs) and External Consultants (frictionless deliverable time reporting via secure link).

### 3. What We Delivered, and What We Left Out and Why
**What we delivered:** Real-time 3-metric budget cockpit (Euros, Person-Days, Hours), server-side German ArbZG § 3 and budget rule enforcement with 43 passing unit tests, the **4-Week Early Warning Reorder Radar** accounting for German holiday shutdown, full English/German (DE/EN) bilingual switcher, proof-of-work attachment viewer, and role-based data minimization (§ 147 AO / DSGVO).  
**What we left out and why:** Minute-by-minute attendance timers (left out to prevent German *Scheinselbstständigkeit* and labor leasing co-employment liabilities) and ERP invoice billing (left out because enterprise payment runs stay in SAP/ERP).

### 4. Price Per User Per Month
